# Role and Responsibilities

**Title:** Deputy Chief Information Officer
**Organization:** University of California, Riverside — ITS (Information Technology Solutions)
**Reports to:** Matthew Gunkel, Associate Vice Chancellor and Chief Information Officer

## Direct Reports

- Disha Kibria, Associate Director of HR/APO Systems
- Brandon Ayers, Associate Director of Architecture and Innovation
- Brian Griffin, Associate Director of ERP Systems
- Juan Gonzales, Associate Director of Campus Web Solutions
- Chuck Forsyth, Associate Director of Research Computing
- Shilpa Upadhayay, Technical Project Manager

## Core Accountabilities

- Operations of all teams listed above
- AI literacy and AI strategy for campus — evaluating tools, speaking to campus groups, guiding faculty toward approved AI tools
- Driving forward initiatives and efforts that don't have a clear owner elsewhere

## Weekly Cadences

- **Monday:** Weekly team leads meeting (Mike updates his direct reports on campus/leadership news; they report back on progress and blockers), followed by ITS Leadership Team meeting with the CIO and peer leaders
- **Ongoing:** Biweekly 1:1s with each direct report (staggered)
- **Ongoing:** Project meetings across initiatives Mike is leading or advising
- **As needed:** Vendor meetings, cross-campus relationship and triage meetings, 1:1 with Matt Gunkel (CIO)
- **Periodic:** Advisory board meetings for three boards Mike chairs — ITS Financial Solutions, ITS HR/APO, and ITS Research

## Quarterly

- ITS Leadership Retreat with peers and CIO for strategic planning

## Routine Decisions

- Vacation approvals
- Review and feedback on documents, proposals, and technical designs from direct and non-direct teams
- Technology procurement approvals and pushback

## Outputs

- Plans and presentations
- Emails — typically either responding to faculty or staff who are stuck or need help, or moving a specific initiative or action forward
- Training materials
- Written analyses and proposals
- Meeting agendas
