# Team and Relationships

---

## Boss

### Matthew Gunkel — Associate Vice Chancellor and Chief Information Officer
**Relationship:** Mike's direct supervisor
**Interaction:** Regular 1:1s, Slack, project-based briefings

**What Matt needs from Mike:** Follow-through on assigned priorities, periodic updates when he needs to brief the Provost, strategic thinking on areas Mike owns, and inclusion on anything AI-related — it's a topic he's personally invested in and wants to be part of.

**What Mike needs from Matt:** Clear direction, roadblock clearing, access to senior UC relationships to move initiatives forward, timely feedback on strategic proposals, and timely approval of procurement and hiring requests.

**Working with Matt:** He asks questions with zero context, making it hard to interpret what he's actually asking. He is not direct — concerns about performance or direction rarely surface clearly. He's a last-minute planner and often overestimates his understanding of technology. He multitasks during 1:1s, so keep conversations focused. He prefers Slack for receiving information; too much detail causes him to lose focus. Emails to Matt should be direct answers without a lot of background context — he doesn't want to wade through setup to get to the point. For formal documents and proposals: lead with a clear problem statement, follow with a direct recommendation, put supporting detail in appendixes. He needs to vet anything before it goes to the Provost. On AI topics especially, make sure he's included and has had a chance to weigh in before things move forward.

**What Rebecca needs:** Advance notice and processing time before she's asked for feedback — give her something in writing and let her sit with it.
