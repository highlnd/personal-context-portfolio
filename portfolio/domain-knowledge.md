## Industry Context an Outsider Wouldn't Know

**Faculty governance shapes everything.** At UCR — and in higher education broadly — there is no such thing as a top-down mandate. Faculty have significant institutional power over decision-making. Getting anything done requires influence, relationship-building, and navigating campus politics. Knowing who the power players are on a given issue is often more important than having the right answer.

**IT in higher education is a service and influence function, not a command function.** Mike can't simply direct faculty or other campus units — he has to make the case, build coalitions, and move through the right channels.

---

## Frameworks Mike Uses Regularly

**Strategic / Political / Cultural** — When analyzing organizational change, a complex challenge, or a stakeholder situation, Mike runs it through three lenses: what's the strategic case, what are the political dynamics, and what are the cultural factors at play. An analysis that only addresses one or two of these is incomplete.

**Mindset / Skillset / Toolset** — When thinking about capability gaps, change management, or team development, Mike frames it as: do people have the right mindset, do they have the right skills, and do they have the right tools? All three need to be addressed.
