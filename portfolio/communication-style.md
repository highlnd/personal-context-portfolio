# Communication Style

## Overall Style

Direct, context-aware, and purposeful. Mike tailors his writing to the audience and the relationship — more concise with people he knows well, more structured when the situation calls for it. He is not formal by default but shifts register when the audience requires it.

On Slack: short and to the point, always.

## Email Structure

Mike leads with context when the topic needs it, then gets to the ask or recommendation. For complex topics, he typically moves through: situation → relevant detail → direct ask or next step. He uses bullet points when listing multiple items, but defaults to short paragraphs otherwise.

He ends emails with either a direct question, a clear ask, or a recommended next step — not a vague close.

## Signature and Salutation

- Always opens with **"Hi [Name],"** or **"Hi Everyone,"**
- Signs off with **"Thank you, Mike"** or just **"Mike"**
- Never uses "Best," "Sincerely," or other formal closings unless the context is extremely formal

## Voice and Tone

- Conversational but purposeful — no filler, no padding
- Uses parenthetical asides for additional context without breaking flow
- Names specific people and references specific documents, links, and slides rather than speaking in abstractions
- Warm when the relationship calls for it, but doesn't perform warmth in professional contexts where it isn't earned

## What Doesn't Sound Like Mike

- Flowery language or purple prose
- Overly corporate phrasing
- Signing off with "Best"
- Long wind-ups before getting to the point
- Generic pleasantries that don't match the relationship

## Context Shifts

- **To direct reports or close peers:** More casual, direct, often short
- **To senior stakeholders or external partners:** More structured, slightly more formal, still concise
- **To Matt:** Direct answers without a lot of background context — he doesn't want to wade through setup to get to the point
- **Formal documents and proposals for Matt:** Problem statement first, clear recommendation, supporting detail in appendixes (this is document format, not email format)
- **Personal emails:** Warmer tone, more personal acknowledgment, still clear and direct
