## 1. Gemini Enterprise Deployment
Deploying Google Gemini Enterprise across UCR for faculty, staff, and students. The approach is experience-driven — focused on specific high-value AI agents rather than a blanket rollout. Core principle: trust is paramount, meaning accurate data and reliable agents from day one.
- **Status:** In progress — go live was end of March 2026
- **Mike's role:** Leading
- **Key collaborators:** Google (close working relationship)
- **Done when:** Secure, agentic AI partner is live and available to campus

## 4. GPAR / Researcher Days
Google Partnership for Accelerated Research — a collaboration with Google Cloud to give UCR researchers access to discounted cloud computing and AI tools. Researcher Days are hybrid on-campus/virtual hands-on training events co-created with anchor tenant research groups.
- **Status:** In progress
- **Mike's role:** Leading
- **Key focus areas:** Co-Scientist pilot (agentic AI for the scientific method), GPU/TPU education, broad Gemini AI sessions for researchers
- **Done when:** Researcher Days delivered, Co-Scientist pilot underway, research community enabled on Google AI tools

## 6. Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance initiative driven by DOJ Title II ADA regulations and UC policy. Deadline: April 24, 2026. A May 2025 audit flagged high risk of non-compliance.
- **Status:** Actively underway — Digital Accessibility Remediation Project in motion, advisory committee and webmaster meetings established, tools being evaluated (BrowserStack)
- **Mike's role:** Leadership/oversight
- **Done when:** UCR websites and digital content meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards

Replacing UCR's legacy timekeeping system (TARS) with UCI's hosted Time Reporting System (TRS), already in use at other UC campuses. Estimated 18–36 months, ~$570K year one, ~$210K annually thereafter.
- **Status:** In planning — business case development, stakeholder alignment ongoing
- **Mike's role:** Leadership
- **Key collaborators:** ITS, Business & Financial Services, HR, APO
- **Done when:** TRS live and TARS decommissioned
