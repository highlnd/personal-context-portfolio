# Tools and Systems

## Daily Stack

**Google Workspace** — The core of everything. Gmail, Calendar, Google Drive, and Google Docs are the primary surfaces for communication, scheduling, and document work.

**Slack** — Primary real-time communication tool across ITS and campus.

**Gemini / Gemini Enterprise** — Used daily for AI-assisted work. Gemini Enterprise is the campus deployment Mike is actively leading; he uses it himself and continues expanding its capabilities as the rollout matures.

**Claude** — Critical to Mike's daily workflow alongside Gemini.

## Task and Focus Management

Mike uses a three-tool system to manage tasks and protect focused work time:

1. **Trello** — Kanban board with four columns: Backlog, Next, In Progress, Done. Anything actionable — from emails, Slack messages, meeting notes, or project tasks — goes into Backlog. Items get promoted to Next when Mike is ready to work them in the coming days.

2. **Google Tasks** — Fed automatically by a Workspace Flow AI that scans incoming email and flags action items. These flow in alongside manually added Trello items.

3. **Sunsama** — Daily planning tool. Each day, Mike pulls from Trello and Google Tasks and time-boxes items directly into his calendar to ensure they actually get done.

## Where Data Lives

- **Google Drive** — Primary home for documents, plans, proposals, and project materials.
- **Gmail** — Secondary source; important context and decisions often live in email threads.

## Tools Being Expanded

- **Gemini Enterprise** — Actively being developed as a more capable collaboration and productivity platform for campus. Mike uses it himself and treats it as a growing part of his workflow.
- **Jira** — Currently in planning; Mike is working with Michelle, Teri, and Jeremy to roll it out for the ITS team.

## Tools Moved Away From

- **Microsoft Office 365, Outlook, OneDrive, Teams, Zoom Chat** — UCR made a campus-wide shift from Microsoft to Google. Mike's personal preference aligns with that move; he avoids these tools even where they still technically exist on campus.
