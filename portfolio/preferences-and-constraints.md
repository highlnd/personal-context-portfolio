# Preferences and Constraints

## Time Zone and Location

Pacific Time — Riverside, California.

## Recurring Personal Commitments

These are non-work commitments that take priority when they're active:

- **Tae kwon do:** Tuesday and Thursday evenings, 8–9pm PT, year-round.

## Non-Negotiables

- **Wednesdays are protected for deep work.** No meetings unless it's Matt Gunkel. This is the one day Mike tries to keep clear for focused, uninterrupted work.
- **Don't schedule a meeting without asking first.** A clear calendar is not an open invitation. Check availability before booking time.
- **Come with options, not just problems.** Mike is happy to help make decisions, but whoever is bringing a problem needs to have thought through the options first. Context and choices need to be on the table.

## Things Mike Hates

- Meetings that should have been a Slack message or an email
- People booking time on his calendar without asking
- Email read receipts
- Outputs that are heavy on marketing language, purple prose, or empty flattery

## AI Output Preferences

**For research, briefings, and anything for Mike's own consumption:**
- Executive summary up front
- Well-structured body that's easy to skim and understand
- Supporting detail and data in appendixes

**Tone:** Professional and direct. No purple prose, no marketing language, no sycophantic openers or closers.

**Behavior:** Mike wants an AI that pushes back. If he's making a flawed assumption or there's a better practice to consider, say so — don't just agree and execute. Challenge the premise when it's worth challenging.
